Data#

This page lists the data sources used across the UK gas and LNG market projects on this site. The references are written in a simple academic style, with links to the original sources where available.

UK gas data#

National Gas Transmission. (2026). Gas Data Portal. National Gas Transmission. Available at: https://data.nationalgas.com/

Used for UK gas-system data, including operational gas indicators, storage information, LNG terminal flow data and balancing-market context.

National Gas Transmission. (2026). System Average Price data. National Gas Transmission Data Portal. Available at: https://data.nationalgas.com/

Used as a public UK gas price reference for analysing short-term gas price pressure.

National Gas Transmission. (2026). Storage stock level data. National Gas Transmission Data Portal. Available at: https://data.nationalgas.com/

Used for UK gas storage context, including Rough and wider long-range storage stock indicators.

National Gas Transmission. (2026). LNG terminal outflow data. National Gas Transmission Data Portal. Available at: https://data.nationalgas.com/

Used for LNG send-out and UK gas supply support indicators.

Power and weather data#

National Energy System Operator. (2026). Data Portal. NESO. Available at: https://www.neso.energy/data-portal

Used for GB electricity demand, wind generation and wider system-context data.

Open-Meteo. (2026). Historical and forecast weather API. Open-Meteo. Available at: https://open-meteo.com/

Used for temperature data and weather-demand context.

Gas benchmark and market-price data#

Intercontinental Exchange. (2026). ICE Dutch TTF Natural Gas Futures. ICE. Available at: https://www.ice.com/products/27996665/Dutch-TTF-Gas-Futures

Used for European gas benchmark price comparison.

Intercontinental Exchange. (2026). UK NBP Natural Gas Futures. ICE. Available at: https://www.ice.com/

Used for UK gas benchmark price comparison.

CME Group. (2026). LNG Japan/Korea Marker Platts Futures. CME Group. Available at: https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/energy/natural-gas/lng-japan-korea-marker-platts-swap-futures.html

Used for global LNG benchmark comparison through the JKM price.

Barchart. (2026). Natural gas futures historical market data. Barchart. Available at: https://www.barchart.com/

Used for downloaded historical futures files used in gas benchmark charts.

Foreign exchange data#

Bank of England. (2026). Exchange rates. Bank of England. Available at: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/boeapps/database/

Used for exchange-rate reference checks.

Yahoo Finance. (2026). Foreign exchange market data. Yahoo Finance. Available at: https://finance.yahoo.com/

Used for practical GBP/USD and EUR/GBP exchange-rate series checks.

Reference texts#

Fanelli, V. (2020). Financial Modelling in Commodity Markets. Boca Raton: CRC Press.

Used as a commodity-market modelling reference.

Hull, J. C. and Basu, S. (2018). Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives. 10th edn. Pearson.

Used as a derivatives, futures and hedging reference.

Nakajima, T. and Hamori, S. (2022). Energy Trading and Risk Management: Commentary on Arbitrage, Risk Measurement, and Hedging Strategy. Singapore: Springer.

Used as an energy trading, hedging and risk-management reference.

Roncalli, T. (2020). Handbook of Financial Risk Management. Boca Raton: CRC Press.

Used as a financial risk-management reference.

Shumway, R. H. and Stoffer, D. S. (2025). Time Series Analysis and Its Applications: With R Examples. 5th edn. Cham: Springer.

Used as a time-series analysis reference.

Hilpisch, Y. (2019). Python for Finance: Mastering Data-Driven Finance. 2nd edn. Sebastopol: O’Reilly.

Used as a Python finance and financial data-analysis reference.

McKinney, W. (2022). Python for Data Analysis. 3rd edn. Sebastopol: O’Reilly.

Used as a pandas and data-cleaning reference.

Software references#

Harris, C. R. et al. (2020). “Array programming with NumPy”. Nature, 585, pp. 357–362. Available at: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2649-2

Used for numerical array processing in Python.

The pandas development team. (2026). pandas documentation. Available at: https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/

Used for data cleaning, time-series alignment and CSV processing.

Matplotlib development team. (2026). Matplotlib documentation. Available at: https://matplotlib.org/stable/

Used for static charts and project visualisations.

statsmodels development team. (2026). statsmodels documentation. Available at: https://www.statsmodels.org/

Used for regression diagnostics and statistical modelling support.
