Commercial Pricing, Forecasting & Commodity Risk Analytics#

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This site is a working portfolio of Excel and Python projects for commercial problems linked to commodity markets.

The focus is not to “price commodities” in isolation. Market prices already exist. The aim is to use market data to price and analyse decisions around spreads, delivered cost, project value, forecast uncertainty, storage, logistics and risk exposure.

Main Project Areas#

UK Gas & LNG Case Study

A case study using UK gas prices, European benchmarks, storage, LNG send-out, interconnector flows and weather demand.

Outputs: spread charts, balance tables, forecast checks, source tables and market interpretation.

View case study

Excel Commercial Model

An Excel workbook for joining market data, building pricing spreads, setting scenario inputs and producing dashboard outputs.

Tools: XLOOKUP, PivotTables, Power Query, LET, LAMBDA, data tables and basic VBA.

View Excel model

Forecasting Study

A Python workflow for cleaning time-series data, testing simple forecasts, comparing model errors and explaining model limits.

Outputs: time-series charts, actual versus predicted plots, forecast error tables and written interpretation.

View forecasting study

Investment and Risk Models

Project-style models for NPV, delivered cost, price shocks, margin sensitivity and commodity exposure.

Outputs: NPV tables, scenario analysis, stress cases, risk summaries and commercial recommendations.

View NPV model

What This Portfolio Is For#

The aim is to build projects that are useful for pricing, commodities, risk, forecasting, logistics and commercial analyst roles.

The work is based around five questions:

  1. What is the market price signal?

  2. What is driving the spread, cost or risk?

  3. What happens under a different scenario?

  4. How reliable is the forecast or model?

  5. What is the commercial implication?

Skills Shown#

  • Excel: XLOOKUP, PivotTables, Power Query, LET, LAMBDA, scenario tables, dashboards and basic VBA.

  • Python: pandas, matplotlib, statsmodels, time-series analysis, model validation and chart generation.

  • Commercial modelling: delivered cost, spread analysis, project NPV, margin sensitivity and scenario analysis.

  • Risk: price shocks, rolling volatility, exposure tables, stress testing and forecast error.
